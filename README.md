## Research interests

I hope to pursue graduate research in **trustworthy and explainable AI**. I want to understand what a model relies on when it gives an answer, what changes after fine-tuning, and when a user should double-check the result. I'd like to study these questions through model evaluation and explore how they connect to AI safety and value alignment.
