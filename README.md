## Research interests

I hope to pursue graduate research in **trustworthy AI**, with a focus on two questions:

- **Interpretability:** Does a model's explanation reflect the evidence behind its answer?
- **LLM evaluation:** How does fine-tuning change what a model gets right, where it fails, and when a person should review the result?

These questions are how I want to approach AI safety and value alignment.
