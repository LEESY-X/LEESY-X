## Research

I’m an undergraduate interested in **trustworthy and explainable AI**. In graduate school, I’d like to study how fine-tuning changes model behavior and how we can test whether an explanation reflects the evidence behind a prediction. I’m also interested in when a system should hand a decision back to a person, and how that question connects to AI safety and value alignment.

## Selected work

**[TRAITHON · Hex](https://kr.linkedin.com/posts/leeseonyeok_traithon-trustworthyaimarathon-trustworthyai-activity-7505159066453893121-AR0G)** · Team lead and AI planner  
We designed a clickbait detection agent around two decisions: what to show when it flags an article, and when to send the flag for human review. My role included planning the system and working through its risks and monitoring requirements with the team.

**Domain-specific fine-tuning · AI+X**  
Worked on a recommendation service for choosing a fine-tuning method by domain. This project led me to ask how to compare adaptation methods when model quality and training cost both matter.

**[Document AI Assistant](https://github.com/LEESY-X/document-ai-assistant)**  
Built a Streamlit interface that retrieves relevant passages and shows the source documents alongside an answer.

**[Solar Wind Forecasting](https://github.com/LEESY-X/solar-wind-forecast)** · Team lead  
Worked on ensemble experiments using solar observations and historical wind measurements. Our team placed **2nd in the 3rd Astronomy & Space AI Competition** after the final code and reproducibility review.

## Teaching

As **AI Team Lead at GDGoC SMU**, I lead a hands-on LLM study group. We start with PyTorch, tokenization, and embeddings before moving on to how language models use them.
